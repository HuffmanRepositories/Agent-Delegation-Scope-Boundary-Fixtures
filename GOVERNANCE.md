# Governance

This repository is maintained as an open fixture corpus. Contributions are reviewed for clarity, safety, scope, and usefulness.

## Principles

- Neutral, implementation-agnostic descriptions.
- Explicit assumptions and boundaries.
- Synthetic-only examples.
- Transparent draft/review status.
- No claims of certification, standards status, or complete coverage.

Maintainers may reject fixtures that introduce sensitive real-world data, ambiguous assertions, unsafe operational guidance, or scope creep into runtime/policy implementation.
