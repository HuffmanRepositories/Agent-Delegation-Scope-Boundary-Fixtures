# Review checklist

- [ ] Is the scenario fully synthetic?
- [ ] Are principal, tenant, resource, action, and scope explicit?
- [ ] Are policy assumptions sufficient to determine the expected outcome?
- [ ] Does the fixture distinguish identity from delegated authority?
- [ ] Does it avoid implying universal token/policy semantics?
- [ ] Is the expected outcome and rationale clear?
- [ ] Is there a positive or negative control where appropriate?
- [ ] Are expiry, revocation, and approval semantics stated when relevant?
- [ ] Is the audit/delegation chain described where relevant?
- [ ] Has the fixture been marked draft/reviewed honestly?
- [ ] Does structural validation pass?
- [ ] Has a human reviewed semantic correctness separately?
